Form 470: Competitive Bidding Deadlines

The Form 470 initiates the competitive bidding process. Key requirements:

  • 28-Day Rule: Form 470 must be posted for at least 28 days before selecting a vendor
  • No Deadline: Form 470 can be filed any time, but earlier is better
  • Best Practice: File Form 470 by early November to allow time for bidding before Form 471 window

Form 471: Application Window

📢 Form 471 Window is OPEN

The FY2026 Form 471 filing window opened January 15, 2026. The exact closing date will be announced by USAC, typically in March. Don't wait until the last minute — early filers receive funding decisions faster.

Form 471 is your actual funding request. Critical reminders:

  • Certify Before Deadline: The application must be certified (signed) before the window closes
  • No Extensions: The FCC does not grant extensions for Form 471
  • Early Filing Benefits: Earlier applications typically receive funding decisions sooner
  • Common Errors: Incorrect FRN references, wrong service start dates, missing documentation

Track Your Funding: Once your Form 471 is filed, you can monitor its progress in real-time using the free FRN Tracker to get instant alerts on USAC decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the E-Rate Form 471 deadline for 2026?

The FY2026 Form 471 filing window typically opens in January and closes in March 2026. The exact deadline is announced by USAC each year. Schools and libraries must submit their Form 471 before the window closes to request E-Rate discounts for the upcoming funding year.

When does the E-Rate Form 470 window open for 2026?

The Form 470 window for FY2026 opened on July 1, 2025. Form 470 must be posted at least 28 days before filing Form 471. Most applicants file Form 470 between July and December to allow adequate time for the competitive bidding process.

What happens if I miss the E-Rate Form 471 deadline?

If you miss the Form 471 deadline, you cannot receive E-Rate funding for that funding year. However, you may be able to file an appeal or waiver request with USAC or the FCC if there were extenuating circumstances. It is critical to track all deadlines and file well before the closing date.

What are the E-Rate invoice deadlines for FY2026?

For FY2026, recurring services (Category 1) invoices are due 120 days after the funding year ends (October 28, 2027). Non-recurring services (Category 2) invoices are also due 120 days after the service delivery deadline (October 28, 2027). Late invoices may require an appeal.

How long do I have to respond to a PIA review?

When USAC sends a PIA (Program Integrity Assurance) review request, you must respond within 15 business days. Failure to respond on time can result in denial of your funding request. Extensions may be available if requested before the deadline expires.
